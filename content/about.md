Hi, I'm Pierre 👋

I work as a **Senior Cybercrime Analyst** at [Sekoia.com](https://www.sekoia.com/), in the
**Threat Detection &amp; Research (TDR)** team.


## What I work on {#what-i-work-on}

<div class="focus">

**Adversary tracking**
: Following cybercrime groups and their campaigns over
    time: tooling, TTPs, and how their operations change.

**Infrastructure hunting**
: Pivoting on C2 servers, delivery chains and hosting
    patterns to map adversary infrastructure, ideally before it is used.

**Malware analysis**
: Reverse engineering loaders, RATs and crypters, and writing
    the configuration extractors, unpackers and YARA rules that come with it.

</div>


## About this blog {#about-this-blog}

This is where I keep my notes from that work: unpacking walkthroughs, obfuscation
tricks, Windows internals and the small scripts that make analysis faster. The code
is meant to be reused, so take it and adapt it.

Every article is written in Org mode in Emacs and exported with
[ox-hugo](https://ox-hugo.scripter.co/). Opinions here are my own.
